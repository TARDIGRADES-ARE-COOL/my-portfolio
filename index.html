01 · About

From the machine up to the model.

I started in mechatronics at Ngee Ann Polytechnic, building and fixing the machines themselves. Now I study Computer Science and Design at SUTD, focusing on AI and cybersecurity.

These days most of my time goes into AI agents: at Fujifilm, and in my own projects. My hardware background helps, because a good agent, like a good machine, has to act reliably in the real world.
